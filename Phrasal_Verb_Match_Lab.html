Three shades of hard work: beaver away suggests busy, diligent, sustained activity (like a hardworking beaver); plug away emphasises persistence despite slow progress or difficulty; slog away suggests exhausting, laborious work over a long period. All three are accepted for the three matching cards in this exercise.
