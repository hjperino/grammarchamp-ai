    Grammar

    G1 · Questions

    do, does, am, is or are?

    Choose the right word. Ask yourself: is there another verb in the question? Then you need do/does. If not, use be.

          Positive, negative, question

              G6 · Present continuous

              What's happening?

              Put the verb in the present continuous (am/is/are + -ing). (–) = negative. Watch the spelling: sitting, making, lying.

                G7 · Simple or continuous? Explain it!

                Choose the form and the reason

                For each sentence, choose the right form and the rule that explains it. In the test you may have to explain your choice, so the reason counts too.

                  Grammar, too

                  Numbers and dates

                  These exercises make new numbers every time you click New set, so you can practise as often as you like.

                  N1 · Write the numbers in words

                  Cardinal numbers (21 → twenty-one) and ordinal numbers (21st → twenty-first). Don't forget the hyphen.

                  N2 · Short forms

                  Write the ordinal number as a figure with its ending: twenty-second → 22nd.

                  N3 · Dates

                  Write how you say the date (28/10 → the twenty-eighth of October) or how you write it (the first of May → 1st May). Months always start with a capital letter.
